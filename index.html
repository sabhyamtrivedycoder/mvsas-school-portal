M

anthan Valley St. Alberts School is a co-education, english medium, secondary school, affiliated with CBSE and located At Balwagarh, Rajdhanwar, Giridih, Jharkhand.

We aim to put children experiences, their voice and their active participation at the center of our curriculum. Our teachers bring the National Curriculum alive by making links between the individual subjects and topics link them with their life in society. The education is intended to enable the optimal development of a child's personality and competencies in line with his/her individual needs and requirements.

Our aim of Education

Mission

We feel that the aim of education should be to develop rational autonomy, sensitivity, democratic and egalitarian values, dignity of labor and skills. We believe that the purpose of primary education is to make the child a self-motivated and independent learner.

Vision

We firmly believe that every human child is capable of learning to live in a society. Learning for them is a discovery of their “self”, the world around them and to share it with their peers, teachers and parents as well. Further in our belief system this learning is a life -long process and we therefore seek to get involve in students, parents, teachers & the society at large, the sheer sense of joy.

Manthan Valley St. Albert School, Giridih

Explore the School

Located in the tranquil lap of nature, our school offers an idyllic environment for learning, free from the distractions of urban life.

Our Modern Class Room

Our Modern Classroom emphasizes interactive learning, critical thinking, and collaboration among students. The use of digital tools, smartboards, and online resources enhances the educational experience, making lessons more engaging and accessible.

Our Innovation Lab

A dynamic space designed to foster creativity, experimentation, and problem-solving. The lab encourages hands-on learning, collaboration, and the development of innovative solutions to real-world challenges.

Our Excellent Libary Room

A resource-rich environment where students can access a wide variety of books, digital media, and educational resources. It serves as a quiet space for reading, research, and study, promoting a love for reading and lifelong learning.

Our Faculty and Staff

Faculty members, including teachers and specialists, are responsible for delivering instruction, guiding students, and fostering their academic and personal growth. Staff members, such as administrators, counselors, and custodians, ensure the smooth operation of the school, supporting both students and faculty in achieving educational goals.
